Evidence boundary

Progress is visible without pretending it is impact.

This site separates artifact existence, reproducible checks, deployment, external interaction, operational outcomes and economic outcomes. The current field work does not claim a KPI change. Technical repositories do not claim adoption merely because they build or test locally. AI assistance is part of the implementation process and does not substitute for independently demonstrated competence.
