Where is the delay?

Missed calls, slow callbacks, owner-only quoting, booking friction, dispatch confusion or stalled follow-up.

What is actually causing it?

I separate the visible symptom from competing causes before anyone buys a tool or adds headcount.

What should we test first?

One bounded change with a clear measurement. If the evidence is weak, the answer can be “do not change this yet.”

Example output

Concrete enough to act on, bounded enough not to guess.

Observed symptom

Paid leads are coming in, but customers often wait for an estimate before they can book.

First uncertain handoff

Qualified request → price/estimate ready for the customer.

Competing causes

Owner approval, missing job details, supplier lookup, unclear pricing rules, or simple queue delay.

Evidence to collect

10 recent leads: arrival time, qualification complete, estimate ready, booked/not booked, and who touched the quote.

Smallest test

Measure quote latency first; if one repeatable lookup/rule dominates the delay, prototype only that step.

Do not change yet

Do not add lead volume, replace the CRM, or automate the whole workflow before the delay is verified.
